# Scrap Meter tutorial

Scrap Meter counts the good (OK) and bad (NOK) parts of your machines, shows
the scrap rate and OEE live, keeps the history and sends alerts to your
messengers. These short pages explain each part of the app with a picture.
The [user guide](../user-guide.md) has every detail.
