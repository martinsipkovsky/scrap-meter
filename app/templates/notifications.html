Alert settings

On: alerts about an idle or stopped station (device offline, job change, scrap) are not sent; the alert log lists them as skipped. "Station stopped, idle or back in production" is still sent, and chat commands always answer.

Providers

A provider is one destination: a WhatsApp group, a Telegram chat, Discord channels, a Signal group, a webhook. Rules pick which providers get their messages. A Discord bot needs Message Content Intent turned on in the Discord developer portal (Bot page) to read commands and the Chat room.

A rule decides what is sent (scrap, faults, production changes, system events), how urgent it is, and to which providers.
