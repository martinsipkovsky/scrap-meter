# Manual entries

Parts counted by hand, for example after sorting or on a machine without a
connection, are added on the station view with **Add entry**: OK and NOK
parts, the time (now, or earlier), the job and a note. It needs the
*manual_entry* permission.

![Station view with Add entry](images/station.png)

A manual entry counts like device data: on the dashboard, the chart, Scrap
statistics, the OEE quality and the chat commands.

**Corrections.** To take back parts counted by mistake, enter a negative
number, e.g. OK −10. If that would take a day below zero the app asks first.
In the Data log you can **Edit** or **Delete** an entry later.

More: [Manual entries](../user-guide.md#manual-entries).
