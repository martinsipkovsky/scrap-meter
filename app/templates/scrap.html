Scrap statistics

How much scrap, where it comes from and how it changes, for any date range.

Pieces are counted from the reading history, like the OK/NOK chart in the station view. Scrap = NOK / (OK + NOK). Pieces from excluded readings, from times a station was not in production (idle or stopped) and from stations set to Exclude from the statistics are left out of the overall figures. Changes compare with the same number of days just before the range. Days follow this browser's time zone ().

Exclude or include a time period
