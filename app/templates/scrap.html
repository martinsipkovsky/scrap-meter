Scrap statistics

Pass, fail and scrap for a date range, overall, per device, per job and per day.

Parts are counted from the reading history, like the OK/NOK chart in the device view. Scrap = fail / (pass + fail). Parts from excluded readings, from times a device was not in production (idle or stopped) and from devices set to Exclude from the statistics are left out of the overall figures. Days follow this browser's time zone ().

Exclude or include a time period

Exclude leaves every reading of the device in this period out of the statistics, for example a test run or a setup with bad parts. Include counts them again, also for a device that is excluded from the statistics by default. Single readings can be excluded or included in the Data log.
